# Towards Data Science — Submission Guidelines

*Sourced from towardsdatascience.com, August 2026.*
*Reference: https://towardsdatascience.com/submission-guidelines/*

TDS moved off Medium in February 2025 and is now an independent publication.
These guidelines define their quality bar — use them to evaluate every article
idea in WRITING_MATERIALS.md before writing.

---

## The core test: is this original and underserved?

TDS explicitly rejects articles that rehash common topics. They will publish
a familiar topic only if it offers "a radical, new take" that distinguishes
it from everything already written. If you can Google the title and find 10
existing articles that say the same thing, it won't pass.

The question to ask before writing: **what does this say that doesn't already
exist?**

---

## What a strong submission has

**Introduction:** must answer three things immediately:
1. What is the novel idea?
2. Why should the reader care?
3. How will you prove it?

If the introduction doesn't answer all three, the article isn't ready.

**Structure:**
- Short, punchy title + longer explanatory subtitle (subtitle appears in
  previews and drives click-through)
- Clear scope — say explicitly what is and isn't covered
- Section headings for visible organisation
- Logical flow with transitions between sections
- A conclusion that reinforces key points (no promotional CTAs)

**Technical depth:**
- Evidence-based reasoning — claims backed by examples, experiments, or data
- Counterarguments acknowledged and rebutted
- Code in code blocks, never screenshots
- Equations with context, not dropped in bare
- Data and images properly licensed for commercial use (web-scraped data
  needs explicit owner permission)

**Writing quality:**
- Action-driven language, precise verbs, no generic filler
- Proofread — spelling, punctuation, grammar
- Reading time: 12–25 minutes for a standard article

**Author:**
- Real name, professional photo, biography required
- No anonymous submissions

---

## What gets rejected

- Redundant topic with no new angle
- Doesn't deliver on what the title/subtitle promises
- Plagiarism or improperly cited work
- Unlicensed images or datasets
- AI-generated or AI-assisted content (TDS does not publish these)
- Anonymous author profile

---

## AI policy

TDS does **not** publish AI-generated or AI-assisted articles. This means
the article must be written by the author. Research, code, and building
can involve AI tools — the writing cannot.

---

## Practical submission rules

- Submit no more than 3 articles at once
- Response time: within one week
- Previously published work (personal blog) accepted but less likely to be
  featured — original submissions preferred

---

## How to apply this to WRITING_MATERIALS.md

Before adding an article idea, run it through these three filters:

1. **Underserved?** Can you find 5+ existing articles that say the same thing?
   If yes, what's the radical new take that makes this different?

2. **Provable?** Can you show it working — code, benchmark numbers, a live
   demo, a trace waterfall? Opinion without evidence doesn't pass.

3. **Specific?** "How I built X" beats "An introduction to X" every time.
   The more concrete and narrow the claim, the stronger the article.

If an idea passes all three, it belongs in WRITING_MATERIALS.md.
If it fails any of them, either sharpen it until it passes or drop it.
